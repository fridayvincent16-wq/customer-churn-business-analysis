# Customer Churn Business Analysis

## Project Overview

This project analyses customer churn using the Telco Customer Churn dataset.

The objective is to identify customer groups and business factors associated with churn and provide actionable recommendations to improve customer retention.

## Business Objectives

- Identify the overall customer churn rate.
- Determine customer segments with high churn.
- Analyse the relationship between tenure and churn.
- Examine how contract type affects customer retention.
- Analyse churn across internet services and payment methods.
- Provide practical recommendations for reducing customer churn.

## Key Findings

- The overall customer churn rate is 26.5%.
- Month-to-month customers have the highest churn rate among contract types.
- Electronic check customers have the highest churn rate among payment methods.
- Fiber-optic customers show a high churn rate.
- Customers with shorter tenure are more likely to churn.

## Recommendations

1. Target customers during their first year with proactive retention strategies.
2. Encourage month-to-month customers to move to longer-term contracts.
3. Investigate the reasons for high churn among electronic-check customers.
4. Investigate the customer experience associated with fiber-optic services.
5. Strengthen customer support and security services to improve retention.
